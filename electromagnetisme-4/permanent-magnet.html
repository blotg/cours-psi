Permanent magnet

We study a magnetic circuit of constant cross-section 𝑆, made of

  • a permanent magnet of length 𝑚=1 cm, whose hysteresis loop is given below;
  • soft iron of length 𝑙𝑒, assumed linear, homogeneous and isotropic, with infinite magnetic permeability;
  • an air gap of length 𝑔=5 mm.

We assume the fields are one-dimensional and neglect flux leakage and edge effects.

1/ What should the hysteresis loop of a good permanent magnet look like? Should a soft or hard material be used?

Coup de pouce 1
Should the hysteresis loop be narrow or wide?
Corrigé
A good permanent magnet needs a wide hysteresis loop, in order to keep a high remanent magnetization. A hard magnetic material is thus needed. The loop given above, with a coercive field of about 900⁠ ⁠kA m−1, is that of a hard material.

2/ Show that the vector 𝐻⃗ is zero in the soft iron.

Coup de pouce 1
Relate 𝐵 and 𝐻 in the soft iron. What is the magnetic permeability of the soft iron?
Corrigé
In the soft iron, which is a soft magnetic material, we have 𝐻⃗𝑒=𝐵⃗𝑒𝜇0𝜇𝑟 with 𝜇𝑟≫1 thus 𝐻⃗𝑒≈0⃗.

3/ Show that the magnetic field is the same in the air gap, the permanent magnet and the soft iron.

Coup de pouce 1
Use the conservation of the flux of 𝐵⃗ through a small closed surface straddling the interface.
Corrigé

By applying the conservation of the flux of 𝐵⃗ through a small closed surface straddling the interface between two media, we have:

𝐵⃗𝑔⋅d𝑆⃗=𝐵⃗𝑒⋅d𝑆⃗

thus

𝐵𝑔=𝐵𝑒

We can apply the same reasoning at the interface between the permanent magnet and the soft iron to get

𝐵𝑚=𝐵𝑒

Coup de pouce 1
