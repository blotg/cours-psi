Permanent magnet

We study a magnetic circuit whose section 𝑆 is constant, composed of

  • a magnet of length 𝑚=10cm described by its hysteresis cycle given below ;
  • electrical steel1 of length 𝑙𝑒 considered linear, homogeneous and isotropic, of infinite magnetic permeability ;
  • an air gap of length 𝑔=1mm.

We suppose the fields to be unidimensional, we neglect any field leak and any side effect.

1/ What should the hysteresis cycle of a good permanent magnet look like ? Should a soft or hard material be used ?

Coup de pouce 1
Le cycle d’hystérésis doit-il être fin ou épais ?
Corrigé
A good permanent magnet needs to have a wide hysteresis cycle, in order to keep a high remanent magnetization. A hard magnetic material is thus needed.

3/ Show that the magnetic field is the same in the air gap, the permanent magnet and the soft magnetic material.

Coup de pouce 1
Utiliser la conservation du flux de 𝐵⃗ sur une petite surface à cheval sur l’interface.
Corrigé

By applying the conservation of the flux of 𝐵⃗ on a small surface straddling the interface between two media, we have :

𝐵⃗𝑔⋅d𝑆⃗=𝐵⃗𝑒⋅d𝑆⃗

thus

𝐵𝑔=𝐵𝑒

We can apply the same reasoning at the interface between the permanent magnet and the electrical steel to get

𝐵𝑚=𝐵𝑒
