Mutual inductance between a wire and a frame

We study an infinite electric wire and a conductive frame. The two objects are coplanar. The bottom of the frame is at a distance 𝑑 from the wire.

Magnetic field created by the wire

By symmetry, the wire is symmetric with respect to the plane passing through point 𝑀 and perpendicular to 𝑒⃗𝜃 (the plane (𝑀,𝑒⃗𝑟,𝑒⃗𝑧)). Consequently, 𝐵⃗(𝑀) is directed along 𝑒⃗𝜃.
