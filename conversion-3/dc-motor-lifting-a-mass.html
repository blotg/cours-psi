DC motor lifting a mass ★★

A DC machine with coupling constant Φ0, internal resistance 𝑟 and inductance 𝐿 is used to lift a mass 𝑀. The axis of the rotor is connected to a pulley of radius 𝑎. A string wound around the pulley carries the mass. The machine is supplied by a generator of electromotive force 𝐸0 and internal resistance 𝑟0.

A friction torque Γ𝑓=−𝑓Ω acts on the rotor. The moment of inertia of the system {rotor + pulley} is 𝐽. Hysteresis and eddy currents are neglected.

The mass is lifted at a constant speed 𝑣0.

1/ Determine the current 𝑖 and the electromotive force 𝐸0 of the generator as a function of 𝑀, 𝑔, 𝑎, Φ0, 𝑓, 𝑟, 𝑟0 and 𝑣0.

Coup de pouce 1
Write an electrical equation and a mechanical equation, and use the relations between the electrical and mechanical quantities of a DC machine.
Coup de pouce 2
For the electrical equation, apply Kirchhoff’s voltage law to the equivalent circuit of the armature.
Coup de pouce 3
For the mechanical equation, apply the angular momentum theorem to the system {mass + string + pulley + rotor}.
Corrigé

Since the mass is lifted at a constant speed, all quantities are constant over time. The inductance 𝐿 will have no influence and is not represented on the electrical schematic.

The electrical equation is obtained by applying Kirchhoff’s voltage law to the electrical circuit:

𝐸0=𝐸+𝑟𝑖+𝑟0𝑖

Moreover 𝐸=Φ0Ω.

The pulley of radius 𝑎 is linked to the rotor, so 𝑣0=𝑎Ω, that is Ω=𝑣0𝑎.

0=𝐽dΩd𝑡=Φ0𝑖−𝑓Ω−𝑀𝑔𝑎

𝑖=𝑀𝑔𝑎+𝑓𝑣0/𝑎Φ0
