Improving the efficiency of a DC voltage source

We want to deliver electric power from a DC voltage source of electromotive force 𝐸 and internal resistance 𝑟 to a resistor 𝑅. We use a controlled switch 𝐾 that works periodically (closed between 𝑛𝑇 and 𝑛𝑇+𝛼𝑇, open the rest of the time).

The values of the components are 𝑟=5 Ω, 𝑅=10 Ω, 𝐸=12 V, 𝛼=0,5, 𝑇=1 µs and 𝐶=100 µF.

1/ For the circuit (a), determine the power received by the resistor 𝑅 and the power delivered by the source during each part of the period. Deduce the efficiency of the device and calculate it.
